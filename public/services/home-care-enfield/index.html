Home Care

Practical support at home can help you or a loved one maintain familiar routines and independence. Cozy Corner Care provides person-centred home care in Enfield and London, with support planned around individual needs and preferences.

Arrange a care consultation

Support with everyday routines

  • Personal care, washing and dressing
  • Meal preparation and support with daily routines
  • Companionship and help to stay connected
  • Medication support agreed in your care plan

How we arrange your care

We begin by discussing the tasks you need help with, preferred visiting times and any routines that matter to you. An assessment helps us agree an appropriate care plan. We review support as your circumstances change.

Our service is managed by experienced NHS nurses. View our Care Quality Commission registration, or meet our team.

Areas we cover

Based in Enfield, Cozy Corner Care supports clients across Greater London, including north and south London, and Hertfordshire. We plan care around your needs and confirm availability for your location. If your area is not mentioned, please call us anyway - we may be able to arrange support. Tell us your postcode so we can discuss availability.

Your questions

Can home care be adjusted as needs change?

Yes. We review care plans regularly and can discuss changes to visiting times, visit length and the level of support.

How much will care cost?

Costs depend on the support required and the agreed schedule. Contact us to discuss your circumstances and receive information about suitable arrangements and pricing.
