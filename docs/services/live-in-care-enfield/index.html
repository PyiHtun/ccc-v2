Live-in Care in Enfield and London

Live-in care offers support in your own home when visiting care may no longer meet your needs. It can help preserve familiar surroundings, relationships and daily routines while providing more consistent assistance.

Arrange a care consultation

A care plan for life at home

  • Support with personal care and daily routines
  • Meal preparation and companionship
  • Assistance with agreed household tasks
  • A plan that takes your preferences and family involvement into account

How we arrange your care

We discuss your daytime and overnight needs, home environment and arrangements for a carer to live in your home. Live-in care does not mean one carer works continuously: rest, breaks and any additional overnight cover must be agreed during assessment.

Our service is managed by experienced NHS nurses. View our Care Quality Commission registration, or meet our team.

Areas we cover

Based in Enfield, we support people across London and Hertfordshire, including Barnet and Potters Bar. Tell us your postcode so we can confirm availability and discuss your needs.

Your questions

Is live-in care the same as continuous awake care?

No. A live-in arrangement includes rest and breaks for the carer. If continuous awake support is needed, we discuss the additional staffing required during assessment.

How much will care cost?

Costs depend on the support required and the agreed schedule. Contact us to discuss your circumstances and receive information about suitable arrangements and pricing.
