Dementia Care in Enfield and London

For someone living with dementia, familiar surroundings and patient, consistent support can be important parts of daily life. We plan home care around the person, their preferences and the routines that help them feel comfortable.

Arrange a care consultation

Support centred on the individual

  • Help with personal care and daily activities
  • Companionship and reassuring familiar routines
  • Support with meals and agreed medication arrangements
  • Communication with family members as agreed in the care plan

How we arrange your care

We discuss the person's history, communication preferences, daily routines and any safety concerns with them and those involved in their care. Support is reviewed as needs change; clinical concerns should be discussed with the appropriate healthcare professional.

Our service is managed by experienced NHS nurses. View our Care Quality Commission registration, or meet our team.

Areas we cover

Based in Enfield, we support people across London and Hertfordshire, including Barnet and Potters Bar. Tell us your postcode so we can confirm availability and discuss your needs.

Your questions

Can family members help shape the care plan?

Yes. With appropriate consent, family members can share routines, preferences and practical information to help us develop person-centred support.

How much will care cost?

Costs depend on the support required and the agreed schedule. Contact us to discuss your circumstances and receive information about suitable arrangements and pricing.
